# Bud Years

Preparation for the tests and competitions that shape your next few years —
built around steady, continuous practice rather than last-minute cramming.

Twenty minutes a day beats a weekend of cramming, because what you revisit is
what you keep.

## What it does

- **A paced daily set.** New material arrives at a rate you can actually keep
  up with, and stops when you have had enough for one day.
- **Everything you get wrong comes back.** Miss a question and it returns —
  answer it correctly on two separate days and it counts as learned.
- **Works offline.** Content downloads once and stays on your device, so a
  train with no signal is still twenty usable minutes.
- **No account, no sign-in.** Nothing to remember, nothing to lose.

## What it covers

SAT®, PSAT/NMSQT® and ACT® preparation, with Vocabulary, Reading & Writing,
Math and Test Strategy — plus Trivia and Logic sets for the days when heavy
practice is not going to happen.

AP®, GRE®, GMAT® and Olympiad material is in preparation.

## Privacy, briefly

Bud Years has no accounts. Your practice history, your streak and your progress
are stored **on your own device** and are never sent anywhere. There is no
advertising, no advertising identifier, and no tracking across other apps.

The full detail is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy), and the
[Terms of Use](/terms) cover the rest.
