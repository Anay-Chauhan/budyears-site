# Support

Email **[info@kalikalyceum.com](mailto:info@kalikalyceum.com)** and a person
will reply, usually within two working days.

It helps to include your device (for example “iPhone 13 Pro, iOS 26”) and the
app version, which is shown at the bottom of the **About** screen. There is no
account, so we cannot look you up — anything we need to know has to be in the
message.

## Reporting a wrong answer or a typo

Tell us the course, the subject, and the number after the **#** at the top
right of the card (for example **#214**), and what you think is wrong.
Corrections reach every device without an app update.

## Common questions

### Where are Settings?

From the **Library**, tap the **ⓘ** button at the top right, then
**Settings**.

### I am not getting the evening reminder

The reminder arrives at 6 PM in your time zone, and only on days when a course
you have started still has work waiting. If **Settings → Evening reminder**
says notifications are blocked, turn them on in your phone's **Settings →
Notifications → Bud Years**.

### I got a new phone. Will my progress come with me?

Your progress is stored on your device, not with us, so we cannot move it for
you. It travels inside your ordinary iCloud Backup: restore the new iPhone from
a backup of the old one and Bud Years picks up where you left off.

### I use an iPhone and an iPad. Why is my progress different on each?

Each device keeps its own record — there is no account to join them up. Both
are real practice, and nothing is lost; each simply shows the work done on it.

### How do I start again?

**Settings → Clear practice history** removes every mark, your review queue
and your progress figures on that device. It cannot be undone.

### How do I switch off statistics or crash reports?

Both are in **Settings → Privacy**: **Usage analytics** and **Crash reports**.
Usage analytics is off unless you have told us you are old enough, and every
feature works the same with either switched off.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Bud Years knows about you is on your device and goes
with it. Anonymous statistics already sent cannot be traced back to you, which
is also why we cannot single them out; they expire on the schedule in the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy).

### Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. You need a connection to download a course's questions the
first time; after that it works offline.
